## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Tone
The design system articulates an efficient, high-trust onboarding experience tailored for e-commerce operators, customer experience directors, and performance agencies. The tone is deliberate, encouraging, and technically sharp. By combining authoritative enterprise utility with ambient, intelligent guidance, the flow transforms complex automation setups (channel integration, AI training, response policies) into a frictionless, predictable progression.

### Design Movement: Modern Corporate Utility with AI Ergonomics
The visual language merges **Corporate Modern** rigor with **Tactile/High-Clarity Utility**:
- **Structured Predictability**: A persistent, unambiguous step progression that visually communicates time investment, requirements, and system readiness.
- **Bi-Chromatic Cognitive Splitting**: Human-configured operational settings anchor in Flow Teal, while autonomous engine configurations, confidence gates, and machine-learning prompts leverage Spark Violet. This maintains continuous mental clarity between platform mechanics and AI behavior.
- **Calm, High-Information Surfaces**: Generous white space within cards balanced against concise typography prevents sensory fatigue while preserving dense operational preview windows.

## Colors

### Hierarchy & Functional Meaning
- **Primary (`#0b7285`)**: Governs core navigation, progression controls ("Next Step", "Save and Continue"), completed step markers, and standard form interactions.
- **Secondary (`#6741d9` - Spark Violet)**: Reserved for intelligent features across the 5 wizard stages—such as automated rule templates, LLM model tuning, intent detection switches, and preview synthesis.
- **Tertiary (`#77440b`)**: Applied sparingly to high-attention caution flags, rate-limit threshold notices, and manual verification warnings.
- **Neutral Palette (`#101d25` base)**: The surface layers leverage tinted blue-grays (`#f5faff` to `#ffffff`) engineered to prevent eye strain during configuration while providing high-contrast accessibility against borders and typography.

### Usage in the 5-Step Flow
1. **Connect Channels**: Primary Teal for authenticated states (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok); neutral outlines for unlinked states.
2. **AI Personality & Voice**: Spark Violet containers emphasize brand persona generation, temperature settings, and preview simulations.
3. **Knowledge Base Ingestion**: Teal for document upload controls; Violet for ingested content vectorization progress bars.
4. **Automation Guardrails**: Amber warnings for human-escalation conditions; Violet badges for auto-resolve criteria.
5. **Simulated Sandbox & Go-Live**: Dual-coded stage displaying simulated customer comments (Neutral), agent oversight (Teal), and automated replies (Violet).

## Typography

### Structural Role
The typography utilizes **Be Vietnam Pro** universally. Its balanced geometry, modern ascenders, and deliberate Vietnamese diacritic engineering ensure crisp legibility across multilingual setups and condensed prompt-engineering textfields.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Step Headings**: Use `headline-md` for step titles and `body-lg` for descriptive subheadings.
- **Form Groups & Section Titles**: Use `title-md` with `600` weight to structure form panels.
- **Inputs, Helpers & Step Indicators**: Form input text and live preview values use `body-md`. Wizard stepper numbering and completed badge tags leverage `label-sm` with tight tracking for clean density.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy & Grid
The onboarding wizard relies on a centered, constrained fluid layout with a maximum content canvas of `1040px` on desktop. This width balances dense split-screen workflows (e.g., configuration controls on the left, instant live simulator on the right) without causing visual disconnection across wide displays.

### Wizard Layout Rules
- **Header & Horizontal Stepper**: Fixed top position with a height of `72px`, bounded by `margin` spacing. The 5-step track distributes evenly across the top axis with flexible connection lines.
- **Dual-Pane Stage**:
  - Left Column (Form Inputs & Configuration): 58% width.
  - Right Column (Live Interactive Sandbox & AI Validation): 42% width.
- **Footer Action Bar**: Docked bottom tray with a height of `68px` providing a persistent anchor for secondary ("Back", "Save Draft") and primary ("Continue", "Test & Finish") triggers.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: Dual-pane configuration with active preview sandbox running alongside form inputs.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: Single centered column (`680px` max-width); sandbox switches to a slide-out preview drawer accessible via a floating action trigger.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Full fluid stack with `margin-mobile` padding (`1rem`). The horizontal stepper condenses into a compact progress bar with a numeric indicator (e.g., "Step 2 of 5: Voice Setup").

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is built through structured surface containment, crisp borders, and subtle atmospheric shadows rather than heavy layering:

- **Level 0 (App Shell Background)**: Surface canvas rendered in `#f5faff`.
- **Level 1 (Wizard Main Card & Panels)**: Grounded on `#ffffff` with a continuous `1px solid #dceaf5` border. Zero shadow to maintain clean focus.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Active Dropdowns)**: Selected channel cards, tooltips, and floating selector popovers use:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(16, 29, 37, 0.06), 0 1px 2px rgba(16, 29, 37, 0.04);`
- **Level 3 (Simulation Floating Sheet & Test Modals)**: Sandbox modal windows and prompt preview dialogs utilize:
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px rgba(16, 29, 37, 0.10), 0 2px 6px rgba(16, 29, 37, 0.04);`
- **AI Highlight Elevation**: AI-focused preview segments and suggestions use an inner tint glow of `inset 0 0 0 1px #b197fc` combined with a soft violet shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(103, 65, 217, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** standard across all elements:
- **Default Interactive Elements (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to text input fields, wizard navigation buttons, checkboxes, radio tiles, and simulated chat bubbles.
- **Structural Wizard Containers (`1rem` / 16px)**: Applied to the main wizard stage envelope, file drag-and-drop targets, and the sandbox preview card.
- **Badges, Step Indicators & Chips (`9999px`)**: Fully circular indicators for the 5-step numbered circles, platform status pills, and AI confidence badges to distinguish them from actionable form controls.

## Components

### Stepper (5-Step Horizontal Wizard Bar)
- **Container**: Flex row, full width across the top card header with `space-lg` bottom margin.
- **Step Node**:
  - **Completed**: Circle (`28px`), background `#0b7285`, checkmark icon in `#ffffff`. Connecting line turns solid `#0b7285`.
  - **Active**: Circle (`28px`), border `2px solid #0b7285`, background `#ffffff`, inner dot `#0b7285` (`8px`), text label `title-sm` in `#101d25`.
  - **Upcoming**: Circle (`28px`), border `1px solid #bec8cc`, background `#ffffff`, step index number in `label-sm` with color `#6f797c`.
- **Connector Line**: Height `2px`, background `#dceaf5`, transitions to `#0b7285` when completed.

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Continue" / "Connect")**: Height `40px`, padding `0 1.25rem`, background `#0b7285`, text `#ffffff`, radius `8px`, font `title-sm`. Hover: `#1098ad`. Focus: outline `2px solid #005868` with `2px` offset.
- **AI Trigger ("Generate Persona" / "Auto-Detect")**: Height `40px`, background `#6741d9`, text `#ffffff`, radius `8px`, paired with a Sparkle icon. Hover: `#7950f2`.
- **Secondary / Back Button**: Height `40px`, background `transparent`, border `1px solid #dceaf5`, text `#101d25`. Hover: background `#f5faff`.

### Channel Connection Cards (Step 1)
- **Container**: Grid layout, 3 columns desktop / 1 column mobile. Border `1px solid #dceaf5`, radius `8px`, padding `space-lg`, background `#ffffff`.
- **States**:
  - Unconnected: Platform icon, channel name in `title-md`, "Connect" button.
  - Connected: Left border highlight `3px solid #0b7285`, "Connected" pill badge in `#e3fafc` text `#0b7285`.

### AI Parameter Controls & Tone Selectors (Step 2 & 4)
- **Radio Selection Tiles**: Multi-select or single-select tiles for tone (e.g., "Professional", "Friendly & Witty", "Direct Support").
- **Unchecked State**: Border `1px solid #dceaf5`, background `#ffffff`.
- **Selected AI State**: Border `1.5px solid #6741d9`, background `#f3f0ff`, title colored in `#6741d9`.

### File Upload & Knowledge Ingestion (Step 3)
- **Dropzone**: Dashed border `2px dashed #b197fc`, background `#f3f0ff` at 30% opacity, radius `12px`, padding `space-xl`.
- **Progress Row**: Linear progress bar height `6px`, fill `#6741d9`, label indicating vectorization status in `body-sm`.

### Live Simulation Sandbox (Step 5 & Global Preview Pane)

- **Mock Feed**: Incoming customer comment bubble in `#ffffff` with neutral border. Real-time auto-generated AI reply in `#f3f0ff` with a left border `2px solid #6741d9` and an AI Spark badge (`#6741d9`).
